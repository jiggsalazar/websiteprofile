About me

Hello! I'm Jigg Salazar, a Website Developer and Designer based in Cebu City, Philippines.

My career started in the BPO industry while working my way through college. Over the years, I had the privilege of supporting businesses across a wide array of sectors—from e-commerce and real estate to finance, publishing, and medical equipment. Navigating these diverse industries taught me how to pivot quickly, solve complex operational challenges, and understand what really makes a business run smoothly.

While managing operations, website design became my go-to creative outlet. It didn't take long to realize that my true strength lies at the intersection of technical structure and creative strategy. Choosing to focus on web development and website design allowed me to bring both worlds together: creating beautifully designed, search-optimized websites and delivering reliable backend support that helps businesses grow.

When I'm Away from the Desk: If I'm not coding or refining workflows, I'm usually in the kitchen experimenting with new baking recipes or out exploring Cebu's mountain trails and scenic beaches. I bring that same curiosity, resilience, and attention to detail to every project I take on.

Whether you need a compelling web presence or a polished website design, I'm here to help turn your goals into results.
